# Credits and licensing

## Original work
- All pixel-art textures and pack icons under `resource_pack/` were generated by
  `tools/generate_assets.py` for this project. They are original and licensed with the repository.
- All scripts, manifests, item definitions, recipes, particle definitions and language strings
  were written for this project.

## Game content referenced by name
- Sound effects (`random.bow`, `random.levelup`, `note.pling`) and vanilla item and effect
  identifiers (`minecraft:slowness`, `minecraft:iron_ingot`, etc.) are referenced by name only.
  No sound or texture files are copied from Minecraft.

## Not used
- No assets were taken from commercial Spider-Man games, films or comics, or from unlicensed packs.
